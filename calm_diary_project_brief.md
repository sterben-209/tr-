# Project Brief: Calm Diary

## Project Overview
**Calm Diary** is a digital scrapbook and mindfulness application designed to provide users with a "quiet space for thoughts." The app emphasizes a gentle, hand-drawn aesthetic to reduce digital fatigue and encourage daily reflection through journaling, mood tracking, and visual personalization.

---

## Design Vision & Aesthetic
- **Theme:** "Scrapbook / Digital Journal"
- **Visual Style:** Hand-drawn borders, sketchy lines, and whimsical illustrations.
- **Color Palette:** Soft pastels (creams, muted yellows, gentle pinks, and sky blues).
- **Typography:** *Plus Jakarta Sans* (clean but friendly) paired with organic, handwritten-style accents.
- **Tone:** Calm, reflective, private, and creative.

---

## Core Features & User Flows

### 1. Onboarding & Authentication
- **Guest Access:** Ability to browse the app and save data locally to the device before committing to an account.
- **Authentication:** Simple, focused Login and Sign-Up flows.
- **Mailbox Binding:** Secure account backup by linking a verified email address.

### 2. The Diary Experience
- **Daily Journaling:** A "Compose" interface that feels like writing on a physical page.
- **Diary List:** A chronological view of past entries with mood indicators.
- **Calendar View:** A monthly grid showing daily mood "stickers" and entry markers for quick historical reference.

### 3. Mood Tracking & Personalization
- **Mood Check-in:** A playful interface for selecting daily emotions (Good Mood, Bad Mood, etc.).
- **Emoticons Store:** A dedicated space to browse and "buy" (restore) various hand-drawn expression sets to customize the diary experience.
- **Mood Analytics:** Visual summaries (e.g., "Mood Tracking" charts) within the user profile.

### 4. Profile & Identity
- **Customizable Profiles:** Users can set nicknames and write personalized "About Me" introductions.
- **Avatar Management:** Hand-drawn avatar frames and easy photo uploads.
- **Engagement Stats:** Quick-glance counters for diary entries and mood frequency.

---

## Technical Specifications
- **Platform:** Responsive Web (Mobile-first design with dedicated Desktop adaptations).
- **Layout:** 
  - **Mobile:** Bottom navigation bar for core features (Calendar, Store, Diary, Account).
  - **Desktop:** Persistent sidebar navigation with centered, high-focus content areas.
- **Frontend Baseline:** Semantic HTML5 and Tailwind CSS for styling.

---

## Target Audience
Individuals seeking a low-pressure, aesthetically pleasing tool for mental well-being, habit tracking, and creative expression. Users who value privacy and "digital minimalism."
